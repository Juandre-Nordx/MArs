# V0.4.1 — Modern Game UI Refresh

- The mission HUD groups Sol, population, credits, browser save controls and simulation speed. Resource cards show quantities, capacities and a text warning for low reserves. New colony catalogue selection is under **Catalogue**.
- Resource artwork renders at 22 px on a direct game load. Construction thumbnails render at 34 px and retain published artwork and the built-in fallback. Map sprites and admin asset previews keep their own sizing.
- The map has a compact view selector and a **Hide inspector** control. Dismissible messages stay in the map. Placement feedback and route actions sit above the construction toolbar.
- The modeless building panel scrolls within the map. Building status, enable/disable and drone production appear first. Power, utility connections, storage and drone assignments are available in expandable sections. Escape closes the panel.
- Construction cards show footprint, metal cost, credit cost and catalogue availability. Active placement keeps its own cost, validity and cancel controls.

The release uses the existing React, Phaser, TypeScript and Fastify workspaces. Schema-8 saves, published catalogue selection, simulation rules and Railway startup retain their existing behavior.
