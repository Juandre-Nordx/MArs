# V0.3.3 — Building information and paid drone queues

Based on main `abc4c0f`, after inspecting the repository and README. No AGENTS.md files were present. Geological scanning, finite local-buffer mining, survey/extraction drone jobs, paid charging, utilities, residents/immigration and central landings were operational. Manufacturing queues were absent; this release extends those systems.

## Building information popup

Click a building footprint or its roster entry to open the same modeless popup. It shows original placeholder artwork, purpose, enabled/health state, actual workers, base electrical load and labelled whole-grid supply/demand/allocation, connected footprint contacts, actual local utility stores/capacity, local mine inventory, assigned drones and continuous production inputs/outputs. Shared cargo is explicitly labelled as shared, with contributed capacity instead of invented local ownership.

The popup scrolls on laptop screens, has a sticky close control and Escape support, and updates when switching buildings. Pointer/click/wheel events inside it do not reach world controls. Uncovered terrain remains available for camera controls. Buildings without discrete products do not show an empty queue interface. Infrastructure controls remain in the existing selected-building sidebar.

## Implemented products

| Producer                 | Product          | Reserved inputs | Assembly | Extra electricity per work tick | Output               |
| ------------------------ | ---------------- | --------------- | -------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Survey and drone station | Survey drone     | 15 shared metal | 12 ticks | 1 kWh                           | One survey drone     |
| Survey and drone station | Extraction drone | 20 shared metal | 18 ticks | 1 kWh                           | One extraction drone |

These are validated configurable starter-metal recipes, not claims of refined industrial supplies. Both require an enabled positive-health station, an electrical connection/allocation and deployment space. There are no technology prerequisites or additional assembly workers for these products. Cargo drones, cargo logistics, refining and other manufactured objects remain unavailable.

Product definitions include IDs, names, descriptions, placeholder icons, producer compatibility, a saved material recipe, duration, extra power, workers/prerequisites and supported output kind/quantity. The registry rejects duplicate IDs, unsupported producers, zero-cost recipes and physical-utility reservations that would need a separate transfer system. Shared limits are data-driven: five orders per station, eight deployed plus reserved drones per station, and 800 deployed plus reserved outputs colony-wide.

## Accounting and operational behavior

Acceptance atomically deducts material inputs into the order's saved reservation and reserves fleet/output capacity. Other construction and queues cannot spend those materials. Selection is saved per producer. Each station advances only its FIFO head. Across producers, order acceptance provides deterministic processing order.

A work tick consumes its full extra electrical demand exactly once, using the existing allocator after base building loads and before drone charging. If a complete work tick cannot be funded, no partial assembly electricity is spent. Base standby power remains a separate existing load. After N successful assembly ticks, the order persists as a completed output awaiting deployment; deployment is attempted on a following operational tick without another assembly charge.

A valid deployment point is an explored cardinal footprint entry free of buildings, residents, buggies and other drones. If deployment is blocked, work pauses and the reservation/output remains saved. Outputs deploy with a unique global ID, the correct station/kind and zero initial charge. Existing paid charging can begin at the entry during the deployment tick; charging is included in actual grid allocation, with no free battery grant. Manufactured drones participate in the existing exclusive survey/mining job system.

Disabling the station, zero health, missing/disconnected power, unavailable required workers or blocked space produces a specific queue reason. Progress resumes after recovery. Game pause and speed settings retain existing deterministic fixed-tick behavior.

Cancellation returns exactly the saved reservation in full even after partial work or completed assembly. Electricity/time are not refunded. If shared capacity cannot hold the entire refund, cancellation is rejected atomically, preserving the order and materials. Repeating cancellation after removal cannot refund twice; completed deployed orders no longer exist.

## Persistence and architecture

Save schema **7** adds product selections, FIFO orders/IDs, material reservations, progress, waiting outputs/block reasons and order/drone counters. Schema 6 adds empty production while retaining existing drones and world state; schema 1–5 migrations continue their original paths. Legacy partial survey timers remain compatible. Current validation checks product/producer compatibility, reservation integrity, progress/output status, order identity counters, unique drone IDs/home station, base drone complements and fleet reservations.

Production is pure simulation code in `packages/simulation/src/production.ts`, separate from React's BuildingPopup and ProductionPanel. Phaser remains a renderer/input adapter. Processing visits active queue heads and building/entity deployment occupancy, not world tiles or all geological deposits. Existing chunk rendering, utilities and production recipes are preserved.

Dockerfile and railway.json are unchanged: no secret-mount instructions, direct Node startup, explicit diagnostics, `/api/health` HTTP health, separate `/api/ready`, and no migrations before HTTP startup. No deployment or production database changes were made.
