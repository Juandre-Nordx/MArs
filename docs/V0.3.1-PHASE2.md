## Resource and discovery balance update

New-game cargo and newly generated deposit reserves are doubled. Initial cargo is water 200 kg, oxygen 200 kg, food 120 kg, energy 240 kWh, metal 160 kg, wastewater 120 kg, carbon dioxide 80 kg and nutrients 160 kg. This all fits existing capacities. Starting credits, production rates, construction costs, extraction rates and mine buffer capacities are unchanged.

Scanner surface reach increases from 0 to 100 m, subsurface from 250 to 500 m. Subsurface is now the UI default and reaches all currently seeded depths. Subsurface/deep scan duration is halved; all modes use half their former scan energy. Positions, seed-defined mineral types, richness and finite-depletion behavior remain unchanged. No new save schema is required: saved inventory and materialized deposits keep their quantities and depletion; only new colonies and newly materialized deposits receive the larger starting quantities. Valid partial scan timers from the earlier slower schema-6 settings are adjusted on load; the final operational scan tick is still required before discovery. Invalid timers outside the old limits remain rejected.

Production and utility-isolation regression fixtures use explicitly partially filled stores to preserve their intended test conditions; the new balance tests separately verify that the doubled default shipment is retained through deployment and reload.

## Controls and behavior

- **Mining** is now an enabled construction category. A Survey and drone station costs 20 metal / 10 credits and includes one aerial survey drone and one ground extraction drone. Their batteries start empty. Connect electricity beside any footprint side; normal utility topology and power allocation apply.
- **Geological operations** accepts inclusive region corners or the current map selection, station and scan mode. Regions are limited to 16×16 tiles within 64 tiles per axis of their station, with up to 16 unfinished surveys. Complete survey history is bounded. Jobs visit every selected tile in deterministic serpentine order. No remote instant discovery occurs.
- **Surface** detects depths up to 100 m in one tick/tile (0.25 kWh); default **subsurface** detects up to 500 m in one tick/tile (0.5 kWh); **deep** detects up to 1,000 m in two ticks/tile (1 kWh). Scanning queries the original seeded records and preserves all remaining quantities, including zero. Deeper scans also detect shallower deposits.
- Terrain mapping is an explicit independent **Map terrain too** option. Scanning without it reveals minerals without changing exploration. Mapping without detection of a buried deposit does not discover it. Camera navigation does neither. The minimap does not color chunks merely because their mineral records were materialized.
- **Geological overlay** marks discovered deposits in the visible chunks: surface mint, buried purple, depth >250 m gold, depleted gray. Click a marked tile or select a discovered deposit to inspect remaining reserve estimate, richness, depth and required extraction technology. The list shows the first 100 discoveries, with the viewport overlay available for the rest. Undiscovered records are not exposed by these UI controls.
- Station and mine status, **Survey jobs and drones**, and each building inspector expose jobs, positions, battery charge and operational shortages. The Storage overview button is beside Save/Load. Its building links also focus the map.

## Extraction rules

All four prototype technologies are available directly; research progression is not implemented. A mine's origin must match an explored, discovered, compatible, nondepleted deposit. Only one mine can claim a deposit. Construction uses the existing paid placement/accessibility rules.

| Building        | Deposit technology | Maximum depth | Rated extraction × richness | Local capacity | Base grid energy/tick | Metal / credits |
| --------------- | ------------------ | ------------- | --------------------------- | -------------- | --------------------- | --------------- |
| Surface mine    | surface            | 0 m           | 2 kg/tick                   | 40 kg          | 0.5 kWh               | 12 / 6          |
| Subsurface mine | underground        | 1,000 m       | 1 kg/tick                   | 30 kg          | 1 kWh                 | 18 / 8          |
| Ice drill       | ice-drill          | 1,000 m       | 1 kg/tick                   | 30 kg          | 0.75 kWh              | 15 / 8          |
| Deep bore mine  | deep               | 1,000 m       | 0.5 kg/tick                 | 20 kg          | 1.5 kWh               | 24 / 12         |

A usable mine needs its base electrical allocation and an extraction drone physically at an accessible footprint edge. Ground drones are automatically assigned to unclaimed jobs within 96 Manhattan tiles of their station. They use the existing deterministic ground pathfinder; buildings block routes, roads influence path preference. Paths are recomputed against current footprints, and blocked drones retain their job and retry. New construction cannot occupy a ground drone's position. Survey drones fly cardinal steps over obstacles.

Each drone has one nullable discriminated job, and save validation rejects incompatible or duplicate exclusive assignments. It moves at most one tile/tick, costing 0.25 kWh from its battery, and an extraction tick costs 1 kWh. Survey batteries hold 128 kWh and charge at up to 4 kWh/tick; extraction batteries hold 64 kWh and charge at up to 2 kWh/tick. Charging requires an operational home station and consumes the actual available grid allocation, after normal building loads, before physical grid energy settles. No battery energy is granted on construction. Drones return to their own station when their return reserve is low, recharge, then resume their saved job; long survey regions can require multiple trips.

Extraction transfers `min(rate × richness, remaining deposit, local room)` from the finite deposit to that mine's buffer. It stops at exact depletion or buffer capacity, or when disabled, damaged to zero health, unpowered, without a suitable drone, travelling, charging or route-blocked. Positive-health buildings follow the existing operational-condition rules.

**Cargo logistics, refining and factories remain pending.** Raw ore and raw ice are stored locally. They never increase usable metal/water or any shared colony inventory. Full buffers cannot currently be emptied. Ground drones can wait indefinitely if a new obstacle makes home unreachable or requires more return energy than they hold; clearing the obstruction permits retry. Station charge rates, battery capacities and scan/extraction definitions are data-driven and schema-validated.

## Storage accounting

- Metal, food, wastewater, carbon dioxide and nutrients remain shared colony inventory. Buildings listed for these resources contribute capacity; the UI does not assign per-building cargo ownership.
- Water, oxygen and grid energy totals are the existing aggregate inventory, backed by actual individual building stores. The physical-store list is a breakdown of those same totals, not additional reserves. Disabled stores retain their contents.
- Local raw mine buffers and drone battery charges are separate. Charged energy has already left the grid. Low/full warnings derive from actual quantities and capacity. Mine and utility-store links open the relevant building inspector.
- During descent, inventory remains landing cargo aboard the Starship.

## Saves and architecture

Save schema **6** adds a strict industry record containing job counter, scan regions/modes/progress/mapping option, station-owned drones with battery/position/activity/exclusive job, and mine/deposit links and local quantities. Current saves retain all existing world exploration, mineral discovery and finite reserve records. Schema 5 migrates by adding empty industry only; schemas 1–4 retain their previous migrations and receive empty industry. Existing buildings, infrastructure, population, routes, buggy arrivals, inventories and positions are preserved. Derived utility networks still rebuild on load under rules version 4.

Validation checks station complements, drone identities/kinds/battery bounds, exclusive jobs, scan region/range/progress, mine/deposit compatibility, unique claims, buffer capacities and conservation bounds. Reloading mid-job is deterministic. Existing network-rule migration tests now use actual legacy schema-5 shapes.

Simulation lives in `packages/simulation/src/industry.ts`; storage selectors live in `storage.ts`. React controls are separate GeologyPanel and StorageOverview components. Phaser renders only visible geological records and drone markers; it does not mutate simulation state. Cached terrain/chunk rendering and fixed timestep remain intact. New art uses original procedural shapes.
